WEB-SLINGER VR

A hand-tracking Spider-Man swinging demo for Quest 3S. Put on your headset, make sure Hand Tracking is enabled in the OS, then hit Enter VR below.

🕸️ Point (index out, other fingers curled) = SHOOT WEB ✊ Fist while web attached = REEL IN ✊ Fist near a building wall = CLIMB (drag hand to climb) 🖐️ Open hand = RELEASE web

Works with either or both hands independently — cross buildings with a double web-swing like the movies.

Desktop preview: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Enter VR on a Quest 3S for the real experience.
